# Spring break photographs and video

Used on the marketing pages for the spring break trip.

## Zemi Miches: the resort's own

Every `zemi-*.jpg` here, and every `spring-*` file in `public/video`, comes from the
"Zemi Miches Digital Assets" library, which David Schwartz at Hilton sent Carter on
3 November 2026:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wVVExjgTMfFTfi4oHMBmSIkVfCEL2IMU

Permission is in writing. Hildebranda Richards, 4 November 2026: "You're welcome to use
the resort images available through the resource library David previously shared. This
will give you access to our official property visuals for your website and social
media." David had asked us to wait for her to confirm before marketing, and that is
her confirmation. It names the website and social media. Ask before using them anywhere
else, such as paid ads or print.

The photographs are used as supplied: made 3200 wide (the smaller ones 2000) and converted
to sRGB, with no color changes. The `-card` files are the restaurant and bar cards on
/spring-break: a 4:5 crop of the original at 1600 wide, because cropping a 3:2 file in CSS
left them soft (Carter, 5 November 2026). The lounge is the right-hand side of its photograph,
which keeps the empty half of the lounge and leaves out the couple in the other half. The resort's brand guide asks for images
that are "colorful, bright, and luminous". The grade we used on an earlier set of these
is gone. Never alter the resort's logo, which the guide forbids. The people in these are
the resort's models: never caption them as Outrider guests.

### Video

Silent loops cut from the library's films, in `public/video`. No grade or added text;
the cuts stop before the resort's logo appears in each film.

The clips are slowed, and stretching 24 or 30 fps footage to that pace left only 16 to 21
different frames a second, which read as lag (Carter, 5 November 2026). So they are a
constant 30 fps, with the missing frames made by Apple's optical-flow frame rate conversion
(VideoToolbox, macOS 15.4 and later). Each shot is interpolated on its own, and a hard cut
stays a hard cut. Same seconds, same pace, same color.

"Hero 4K" is ZemiMiches_Hero_Video_4K.MP4, "Hero 60" is ZemiMiches_Hero_60s_wide_PUJMIQQ2026.mp4,
and "Destination" is ZemiMiches_Destination_Video.MP4. Each clip also has a `-phone` cut, 720x960
(the hero 720x1280), from the same seconds unless the table says otherwise.
